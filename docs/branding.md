# UniArena branding

Selected direction: facing flashcards with a lightning-bolt gap, standing in an oval arena (indigo on the left, mint on the right). The logo is transparent and shown without a box; see [Arena logo](../specs/tasks/arena-logo.md). Indigo #4F46E5 for primary actions, mint #6EE7B7 for decorative highlights, navy #17223B for text, white cards on #F7F8FC. Mint backgrounds require dark text. The generated transparent logo is in assets/uniarena-icon.png.

Theme, background, icon and layout requirements are grouped in [Appearance and Navigation](../specs/appearance-and-navigation.md).
