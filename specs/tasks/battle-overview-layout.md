# Battle overview layout

Parent topics: [Battle Mode](../battle-mode.md) and [Appearance and navigation](../appearance-and-navigation.md).

## Why

The wide, ungrouped Battle overview made it difficult to scan available actions and compare ongoing, pending and completed Battles.

## What

Battle Mode opens with a compact indigo-to-mint summary hero. It shows durable Battle XP, the win/loss record, and the signed-in player's real global Leaderboard rank, plus **New Battle**. If the read-only Leaderboard is temporarily unavailable, the rank is omitted while Battle remains usable. Active battles and Battle invitations occupy separate lanes, with Recent results beneath them. Active cards show the deck, matching state, deadline and Resume action when the player still has questions. Invitations identify the sender, deck and available response. Results show the two scores, outcome, XP change and review action.

## Out of scope

Leaderboard ranking, new Battle data, matching rules, scoring, timing and session actions.

## Done when

1. The hero shows the profile's Battle XP, persisted win/loss record and real Leaderboard rank without invented statistics.
2. New Battle, Start/Resume, Cancel search and Review trigger their existing actions.
3. Active battles, Battle invitations and Recent results remain correctly separated in the compact overview.
4. The overview is readable in light and dark themes and collapses cleanly on narrow screens.
5. Incoming invitations are recognisable as new messages without changing their Accept or Decline behaviour.
