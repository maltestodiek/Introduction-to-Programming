# Study round layout

Parent topics: [Study Mode](../study-mode.md) and [Appearance and navigation](../appearance-and-navigation.md).

## Why

Students should see their position and own learning outcomes while answering without losing space for question tools, notes or feedback.

## What

The active Study round uses the same two-column arrangement as Battle: question and answers on the left, and a right-side overview of every question on the right. The overview marks correct, incorrect, current and unopened questions from the student's persisted answers. Study has no countdown because its approved workflow is asynchronous and has no per-question timer.

## Out of scope

Changing Study scoring, matching, deadlines, notes, feedback privacy, Battle timing, or another participant's private answers.

## Done when

1. An active Study question displays the question and answers beside a progress overview.
2. The overview distinguishes correct, incorrect, current and unopened questions.
3. A reopening student sees their saved answer states in the overview.
4. Another student cannot read the session overview data.
