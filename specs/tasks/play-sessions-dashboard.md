# Play sessions dashboard

Parent topic: [Appearance and navigation](../appearance-and-navigation.md).

## Why

Ongoing sessions and history were two equal Battle/Study columns of large cards. Students could not see at a glance what to do next, whose turn it is or how they are doing.

## What

Play starts directly with the Battle and Study mode cards; it has no separate "Create new session" heading or introductory sentence. Battle sessions, results and figures live only in Battle Mode. Below the cards, **Your study sessions** presents the student's active and completed Study sessions in two columns.

The main column starts with a **Continue where you left off** banner for the next Study session, preferring a Together Study session with the nearest deadline. Its Resume button does exactly what the session's own resume button does. **Ongoing study sessions** follow as a two-column grid of cards with progress, a turn badge, the partner menu for Together sessions, deadlines and the existing Open/Resume Study action. **Study history** is a table with Result, Deck, Mode (Solo/Together with the partner menu), Score and XP plus the existing View Study session action; it shows five rows and Show more adds ten.

The side column shows finished Study sessions with answered questions and the existing Study plan to-dos.

Only stored data is shown. Sessions have no stored finish date and Study sessions no stored accuracy, so the table has no Date column and the figures use counts instead of weekly XP or accuracy.

## Out of scope

New session data (dates, accuracy, weekly XP), Battle or Study rules, and a separate New session button (the creation area above already starts sessions).

## Done when

1. The continue banner, ongoing cards and history rows resume the corresponding Study session.
2. No Battle session, result, Battle XP, record or streak is shown outside Battle Mode.
3. Turn badges match the Study session state, and history results show Done or Expired.
4. History shows five rows first and more on request; empty states invite starting a session.
5. The page works in light and dark themes and keeps the mode-card creation area above the dashboard without redundant introductory copy.
