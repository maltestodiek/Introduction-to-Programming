# Play sessions dashboard

Parent topic: [Appearance and navigation](../appearance-and-navigation.md).

## Why

The Play landing page should be a focused choice between Battle Mode and Study Mode, without a dashboard that distracts from those two entry points.

## What

Play starts directly with the Battle and Study mode cards; it has no separate "Create new session" heading, introductory sentence or dashboard beneath the cards. Battle sessions, results and figures live only in Battle Mode. After selecting Study Mode, the dashboard lists each ongoing session once, with its resume or open action. Ongoing cards and history rows identify Study alone or Study together. Each setup screen lists its ongoing sessions under Continue session. Study alone places this list below today’s study plan and above the button for creating a new session. Finished sessions remain accessible in Study history. Figures and Study Plan to-dos remain available. Study Mode does not show a secondary “Change play mode” control; the left-side Play navigation returns to the two-card Play landing page.

## Out of scope

New session data (dates, accuracy, weekly XP) and Battle or Study rules.

## Done when

1. Play renders only the Battle Mode and Study Mode cards before a mode is selected.
2. No Study-session dashboard, Study Plan to-do, Battle session, result, Battle XP, record or streak appears below the mode cards on the initial Play landing page.
3. Each active session has one card. Active and historical sessions show their study mode even when partner details are unavailable.
4. The page works in light and dark themes without redundant introductory copy.

Study alone and Study together open on their own menus. Create a new study session opens the settings on a separate screen. Matched session cards place the partner portrait and name together, with direct friendship and message icon buttons. Completed cards show the mode and correct answers out of the session total. Opening history is read-only and Back returns to history. Reviews use the student’s saved answers and the original question snapshot.

The Study dashboard and each mode menu group cards under Your turn, Waiting and Completed. Waiting distinguishes looking for a match, an outstanding friend invitation and finishing before a matched partner. Only a settled match belongs in Completed. All screens use the same server-side 48-hour expiry and scoring rules.
