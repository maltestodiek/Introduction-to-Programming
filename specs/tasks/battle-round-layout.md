# Battle round layout

Parent topics: [Battle Mode](../battle-mode.md) and [Appearance and navigation](../appearance-and-navigation.md).

## Why

Players need to see their position, answer outcomes and the remaining server-owned question time without leaving a Battle.

## What

The active Battle round places the question and four answers in a two-column answer layout on the left. A right-side overview shows all 15 question states: correct, incorrect or timed out, the current question, and unopened questions. A visible 30-second countdown bar updates from the server-provided remaining time. The overview reads the current user's persisted answer states, so it remains correct after reopening a Battle.

## Out of scope

Changing Battle scoring, server timing, matching, ratings, Study progress, or any other participant's private answer data.

## Done when

1. An active question shows a shrinking time bar derived from the server's remaining seconds.
2. The overview shows 15 positions, with correct answers distinct from wrong or timed-out answers.
3. A reopening player sees their saved answer states in the overview.
4. A non-participant cannot read the round overview data.
